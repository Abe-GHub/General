A 100-point engagement rubric

Rate each firm 1–5 on all six criteria. Multiply each rating by its weight, divide by five, and add the results.

EVIDENCE

What the pilot could—and could not—verify

Important limits

  • No firm publicly named Express Scripts, CVS Caremark, OptumRx, Prime Therapeutics, MedImpact, or Navitus as a placement client.
  • PBM relevance therefore rests on practice-area statements, pharmacy focus, or payer/managed-care history—not disclosed client mandates.
  • Only WittKieffer, Furst Group, and Direct Recruiters homepages were read live. Other profiles are web-index sourced and should be rechecked before outreach.
  • Where only a press, marketing, or executive email was found, it is marked “best available,” not candidate-facing.
